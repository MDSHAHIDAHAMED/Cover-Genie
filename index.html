QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Is Cover Genie free?

Yes. Cover Genie is a free assignment and lab report cover page maker. There is no sign-up and it runs in your browser.

What can I download?

An A4 (210×297 mm) PDF or a high-resolution PNG of your cover page, plus an optional lab report index page.

Can I add my university logo?

Yes. Choose your university from the built-in logo directory or upload your own PNG or SVG.

Do I need MS Word?

No. Fill in your details, pick a template and download the finished cover page directly.

Does it support Bangla?

Yes. You can switch the cover language to Bangla, and the cover uses a Bengali font.
